how to get an access token (1 minute)
  1. Open Canvas in a browser and click Account → Settings.
  2. Scroll to Approved Integrations and click + New Access Token.
  3. Purpose: Jiko. Pick an expiry date after the end of term, or leave it blank.
  4. Click Generate Token, copy the long token, and paste it below. Canvas shows it only once.
