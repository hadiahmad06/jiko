# Field notes

Sprint 3 item 2: two people run Jiko for five days against the deployed
backend. This file is the input to item 6 — which presets get cut.

**Write down what was actually said, not a summary of it.** "morning_checkin is
noisy" is an opinion and tunes nothing. "it asked what I was working on at 7am
on a Saturday, I don't have a job" names the condition that was wrong. Ten
verbatim reactions is the bar in the definition of done.

Nudges can also be marked wrong in the app — one tap sets
`metadata.feedback = 'wrong'`, and three wrong verdicts on one trigger inside
seven days disable it automatically (D32). Do both: the tap is what the system
learns from, this file is what *you* learn from.
